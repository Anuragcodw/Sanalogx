Import Rules
  • Category_Master — creates or maps categories. Existing categories are never overwritten.
  • Medicine_Import — creates new medicine master records (PENDING_REVIEW until approved).
  • Starter_Examples — created as draft records only. Require pharmacy review before activation.
  • Does not create batches, stock, invoices or transactions.
  • Never invents barcodes, prices, batch numbers or expiry dates.
  • Exact duplicates (generic + strength + dosage form) are detected and skipped — existing records are preserved.
